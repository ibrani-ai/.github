# עבראני (Ibrani) — AI practice for spoken Hebrew, for Arabic-speaking students in Israel

**[Ibrani](https://www.ibrani.ai)** (Hebrew: עבראני, Arabic: عبراني) builds AI tools that let Arabic-speaking students in Israeli schools (grades 7–12) practice spoken Hebrew and prepare for the oral Hebrew Bagrut exam, questionnaire **14386 / 15386** (עברית דבורה). The company is Tap Systems Ltd (ט.פ מערכות בע״מ), founded 2024 in Israel. *Last updated: 9 November 2026.*

## Products

### עבראני תרגול (Ibrani Tirgul) — for teachers and classes
[www.ibrani.ai](https://www.ibrani.ai). A teacher opens a class, students speak Hebrew from a phone or computer in the browser (no app, no student account), the recording is transcribed and scored out of 100 on four measures: **content, linguistic correctness, fluency, vocabulary**. The teacher sees every student's result on one dashboard, usually within a minute. Ready-made exercises by CEFR level (A2–B2) or teacher-written ones.

**Bagrut mode (מצב בגרות)** runs a class-wide simulation of the oral Bagrut: 3 sections, 5 questions (two on a personal topic, one chosen from two, two on a short video), 30 minutes, 2 recordings per question, no hints or corrections. Details: [www.ibrani.ai/bagrut](https://www.ibrani.ai/bagrut).

### עבראני בגרות (Ibrani Bagrut) — for students and parents
The same simulation for students preparing on their own. See [www.ibrani.ai](https://www.ibrani.ai) for availability.

## Facts

- Interface languages: Hebrew and Arabic.
- Exam covered: oral Hebrew Bagrut for Arabic speakers, questionnaire 14386 (Arab sector) and 15386 (Druze and Circassian sector), the same exam under two numbers.
- Official rubric (Ministry of Education): content 40%, pronunciation and fluency 20%, structure and correctness 20%, vocabulary 20%.
- The practice tool is approved by the Israeli Ministry of Education and can be purchased through the Gefen program.
- Pricing: one pilot class per teacher free for three months, then an annual school plan priced by number of classes. [www.ibrani.ai/pricing](https://www.ibrani.ai/pricing)
- Not affiliated with "עבראניאת" (Ibraniyat), a Ministry of Education program, or with ivrit.ai.
